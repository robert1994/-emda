How to play Ħemda

Ħemda is an ambient synth: you play notes, or let it play itself, and shape the sound with four encoders under the screen. Everything here also works in the browser version, which runs the same firmware.

Start in one minute

  1. Power. Plug a USB-C charger into 5V/PROG and slide the power switch. Plug headphones into PHONES, or a mixer into OUT.
  2. Volume. Turn the MASTER knob up a little. All the way down is mute.
  3. Make sound. Tap PLAY and Ħemda plays by itself in the chosen scale. Or plug in a keyboard (USB, MIDI or Bluetooth) and play.
  4. Pick a sound. Turn the LINE 1 knob. A small list shows where you are, and the sound switches when you stop turning. Or push encoder 1 on the INSTRUMENT card for the full list.
  5. Add space. Turn the LINE 2 knob for more reverb.

Knobs and buttons

MASTER knobOutput volume.
LINE 1 knobInstrument. With PLAY held: input 1 level.
LINE 2 knobReverb. With PLAY held: input 2 level.
4 encodersEach one changes the big value on the card above it. Push and turn changes the card's other row. A push alone switches an on/off card.
ROW UP / ROW DOWNMove through the rows and pages: top row, bottom row, next page. Press both together for the big landscape view.
LATCHHolds the notes you play, so your hands are free.
PLAYĦemda plays by itself, on or off.
MENUPresets and settings. The sections open and close with a push of encoder 1.

The screen helps as you go: a card lights up when you turn its encoder, and the bottom line shows the preset in use (P1 to P8) with a dot when you have changed the sound since saving.

After you load a preset a Line knob may not match the sound. Turn it past the shown value and it takes over, so nothing jumps.

The pages

Each page has four controls on the top row and four on the bottom row.

SOUNDInstrument, Warmth, Colour, Synth level
Density, Scale, Reverb, Width. Density sets how busy the self-playing is. There are 16 scales.
QTARQtar on/off, Intensity, Drop length, Pad/Qtar
Scatter, Drop memory, Reverb, Width. Qtar adds falling drops of notes over the pad.
SHAPEAttack, Decay, Sustain, Release
Fade in, Fade out, Hold, Rest. Push encoder 1 for a slow swell: off, once or looping.
REVERSEReverse on/off, Window, Mix, Feedback
Attack, Release, Width, Reverb. Plays what you just heard backwards.
GLITCHGlitch on/off, Rate, Slice, Mix
Chance, Scatter, Reverse %, Repeats. Chops and repeats the sound.
ANALOGVibrato, Movement, LFO rate, Filter envelope
Detune, Drive, Warmth, Colour. For the analog-style sounds.
ADVANCEDRing mod, FX, FX mix, FX rate
Arp, Arp rate, Arp octaves, Arp direction. Also for the analog-style sounds.

Hold a button (1.5 seconds)

Hold ROW UPMIDI learn: link a knob on your keyboard to a Ħemda control.
Hold ROW DOWNSave the sound over the preset you are on. The screen says which one.
Hold LATCHINPUT LAB: sampler, follow and freeze for what you plug in.
Hold PLAYThe effects and line input pages.
Hold MENUUpdate from a USB stick.

Every press and hold can be given a different job in Menu > Knobs + Buttons. To leave INPUT LAB or the effects pages, hold the same button again or press MENU.

Sounds

  • Five originals: Velvet Pad, Soft Tines, Tape Choir, Mello Flute, Tape Strings.
  • 32 analog-style sounds with three oscillators each: pads, pianos, organs, basses, leads and flutes. The ANALOG and ADVANCED pages shape them.
  • Sampler: record a sound into input 1 or 2 and play it on the keys.

Arpeggiator

On the ADVANCED page, turn ARP on and hold a chord: Ħemda plays the notes one after another. HOLD keeps it going after you let go. Some sounds start with the arpeggiator on. The page shows the tempo and blinks on each step. Set the speed in Menu > MIDI > Arp tempo, or send MIDI clock and it follows your drum machine or computer.

Effects (hold PLAY)

GEN LOSSWorn tape: wow, flutter, saturation, hiss and dropouts.
TAPE ECHORepeats that fade and darken.
SHIMMERA reverb that climbs in pitch into a bright cloud. HOLD freezes it.
TEXTUREThe character of the reverb: smooth, sparse or sparkle, with slow movement.
LINESLevels: input 1, input 2, the synth, line out, headphones and input gain.

Inputs (hold LATCH for INPUT LAB)

Plug a guitar pedal, phone, microphone preamp or another synth into IN 1 or IN 2. It goes through Ħemda's reverb, delay and tape.

SAMPLERPush encoder 1 to record, push again to stop. Then play the recording on the keys.
FOLLOWĦemda listens to the note you play or sing and adds harmony, or sends it out as MIDI.
FREEZECatches a moment of the input and holds it as a cloud of grains.

MIDI and Bluetooth

  • USB keyboard: plug it into the KEYBOARD socket. It gets power from Ħemda.
  • MIDI IN / OUT: standard 5-pin sockets. OUT can pass on what comes in (MIDI thru).
  • Bluetooth MIDI: Ħemda shows up as “Ħemda XXXX” on an iPad, Mac, PC or Android device.
  • Bluetooth controllers: Menu > Bluetooth > Connect a BT controller finds controllers such as a Seaboard Block.
  • MPE: with an MPE controller, each note bends, swells and slides on its own.
  • MIDI clock: sets the arpeggiator tempo.

Presets

There are 8 presets. Hold ROW DOWN to save the sound you have now. To load one, or to save to a different slot, open Menu > Presets. The list shows which instrument each preset uses. Presets and settings can be copied to a USB stick from the same menu.
