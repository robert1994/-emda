Ħemda on the M-VAVE FM1

M-VAVE opened the FM1's firmware to developers in October 2026. We're adapting Ħemda to run on it: Ħemda's instruments, effects and pages, played from the FM1's own keys, encoders and 240×240 screen.

StatusIn development. No firmware to download yet.
What changesThe FM1 has much less memory than Ħemda’s own board, so some of Ħemda’s longest effects may be shorter or left out.
Your FM1You'll be able to go back to M-VAVE's own firmware at any time with their workbench.

Ħemda is an independent project by Robert Farrugia. It is not made or endorsed by M-VAVE. FM1 is M-VAVE's product name.
